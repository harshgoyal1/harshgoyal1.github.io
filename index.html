SIGNATURE PLATFORM · MULTI-CLOUD · ROLE: ARCHITECTURE, DESIGN & BUILD

CloudOps360 — autonomous cloud operations

The problem. Cloud operations ran on tickets and people: an alert fired, an engineer read it, decided, and typed the fix. Remediation cycles took days and every action was an unlogged judgement call.

The design call. Let AI analyse and propose, never act unsupervised. Every remediation is snapshot-protected and approval-gated, every step is audited, and the same control plane covers inventory, policy and compliance, security, patch management and natural-language-to-Terraform provisioning across Azure and AWS.

  • Control plane: unified estate visibility, compliance state, security posture and patch status
  • Remediation loop: Alert → AI analysis → decision → human approval → remediate → validate → audit
  • Provisioning: natural language to Terraform, reviewed before apply
  • Identity: Entra-authenticated API, scoped admin roles, per-tenant isolation
⚡ Remediation cycles compressed from days to hours · every action explainable
